The game needs its files from the LEGOLAND CD. Choose the CD's image (an .iso, or a .bin), from any language's CD. It's copied into this browser's storage for this site, installed there, and kept for your next visits; nothing is uploaded.

The game is installed in this browser.

For the music, the game needs General MIDI instruments: Windows' gm.dls (in C:\Windows\System32\drivers on any Windows PC; it sounds as the original), or a General MIDI SoundFont (.sf2).

The LEGOLAND port: the 1999 game, decompiled. You need your own copy of the CD.
